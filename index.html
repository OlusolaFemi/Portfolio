Process

How I work.

A five-stage process refined across every product engagement.

01
Discover & Define

Problem interviews with end-users, competitive landscape mapping, and pain point quantification before writing a single requirement.

02
Scope & Architect

MVP prioritization using TVL, developer activity, or usage data. SLA definition, API schema design, and compliance architecture embedded from day one.

03
Build & Ship

Full Agile delivery — Sprint Planning, Stand-ups, Reviews, Retrospectives — with groomed backlog, clear acceptance criteria, and UAT ownership at every release.

04
Measure & Iterate

Instrumented KPI tracking from day one. Monthly insights delivered to stakeholders. Data used to drive product decisions, not just report on them.

05
Expand & Scale

Ecosystem grant strategy, design partner programs, and community engagement to build the distribution that a great product deserves.
